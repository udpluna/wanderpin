# Privacy

**WanderPin runs entirely on your computer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.**

- Your **locations, saved places, routes, schedules and history** are stored only on your computer (`%APPDATA%\WanderPin` on Windows). They're never sent to the WanderPin authors.
- **Maps** are loaded from OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap and Esri. **Search** uses Photon (komoot), with Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) as a fallback. **Road routes** use the FOSSGIS OSRM servers. These services receive the map area, search text or route points you request, plus your IP address, under their own privacy policies.
- **Update check:** on startup the app asks GitHub for the latest release version.
- **Phone control** only works on your own Wi-Fi network and is protected by a private access key.
- **Donations** go through Buy Me a Coffee, a separate website with its own privacy policy.
